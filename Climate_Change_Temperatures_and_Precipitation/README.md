This project analyzed long-term climate data, primarily focusing on temperature trends in Phoenix, Arizona, and drought conditions in the U.S. Southwest. The temperature analysis of Phoenix from 1900 to 2021 showed a statistically significant increase in average maximum temperatures, particularly during summer months, since the early 1960s, supporting the claim of modern global warming. A statistical hypothesis test on precipitation data for 13 Southwest cities confirmed that the EPA's designated drought periods (2002-2005 and 2012-2020) had a significantly lower average annual precipitation compared to other years, validating the classification of these periods as abnormally dry.
